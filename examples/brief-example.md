# Brief: online study-room booking for the town library

## The problem
Members book the library's six study rooms by phoning or visiting the front desk. At exam time the
desk is overwhelmed, and around a quarter of booked rooms sit empty because people don't turn up.

## What I want
A way for members to see which rooms are free and book them themselves, with fewer empty rooms.

## What I know
- About 4,000 active members; roughly 300 room bookings a month, peaking in May and December.
- We are a public library in England, so the service must meet public-sector accessibility rules.
- We already use a library management system for member accounts.

## What I need from you
Research how other libraries handle this and what members expect, then give me requirements I can
take to suppliers, with clear priorities and acceptance criteria.
